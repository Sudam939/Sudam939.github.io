Why Laravel

Laravel gives you a mature, well-documented framework with built-in authentication, queues, validation and a large ecosystem. For admin-heavy, data-driven systems it usually means faster delivery and easier maintenance than assembling a stack from scratch, and it’s easy to find developers who can take over later.
