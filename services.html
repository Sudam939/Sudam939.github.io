How a project runs

  1. Discovery. We agree on goals, users and must-have features.
  2. Scope and quote. You get a written scope, timeline and price before work starts.
  3. Build in milestones. You see working versions early and can change direction cheaply.
  4. Launch and handover. Deployment, documentation and a walkthrough for your team.
  5. Support. Fixes and improvements after launch.
